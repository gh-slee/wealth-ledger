# WL-P0-012E: Build the V2 Exposures page

## Goal

Exposures is the approved child of Stock/Bond: top companies through ETFs, and sector, country and region distributions with visible coverage. WL-P0-010 delivers the exposure data contract; no ticket builds the page.

## Context

### Current behaviour

- The V2 shell exists (WL-P0-011A, `frontend/src/v2/shell.tsx`) and shows "Enable a source" on Exposures until WL-P0-011H lands. The Stock/Bond page and shared analysis toolbar come from WL-P0-012B.
- Nearest shipped page: Exposure (`frontend/src/components/analysis.tsx:294-420`), which already shows every represented country (WL-P0-001) and a chart data table. Observed 2026-11-10: headings repeat as captions; there is no denominator choice.
- Data: the exposure endpoint from WL-P0-010B.

### Expected behaviour

- Route `/app/investments/exposures`, reached from the Stock/Bond navigation child.
- Top 20 companies with rank, direct, through-ETF and total share of the stated denominator.
- Sector, country and region distributions, each as a full-height chart with a table; every represented country is reachable; non-country buckets are separate rows.
- Coverage, unresolved share and issuer effective and retrieval dates are visible. A denominator switch between securities and total.
- Loading keeps the page structure and never shows 0 before a response. Empty, partial, stale and error states each state what is missing and offer one next action.

### Scope

- In scope: the page, locale keys, tests.
- Out of scope: ETF search for unheld funds (WL-P1-002), overlap analysis (UX-P1-004), the DTO (WL-P0-010).

### Implementation notes

- Reuse `frontend/src/components/data-table.tsx`, `chart.tsx`, `ui/button.tsx`, `lib/format.ts`, `lib/i18n.tsx`, `lib/theme.tsx` and the tokens in `frontend/src/styles.css`. Read [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md), [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) and [web-frontend.md](../docs/web-frontend.md).
- Browser tests intercept `/api/v2` with Playwright `page.route`, as `frontend/tests/browser/onboarding.spec.ts` does for `/api/v1`, until a synthetic V2 fixture exists.
- Add locale keys to all three catalogs in one block named for this ticket; rebase before the PR.
- Port the complete-country chart behaviour and its tests from `frontend/tests/browser/country-exposure.spec.ts`; do not reintroduce a fixed country list or an unexpandable "Other".
- Unresolved ETF branches stay "unknown"; covered weights are never rescaled to 100%.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given the golden exposure fixture, then US, TW, unknown and cash amounts match and sum to the denominator.
- [ ] Given 31 represented countries, then all 31 are reachable in the chart and present in the table at 375 px and 1024 px.
- [ ] The top-20 table ranks 1 to 20 with direct, through-ETF and total columns.
- [ ] Given an unsupported ETF, then its weight appears as unresolved and coverage is below 100%.
- [ ] Switching the denominator updates every percentage and the stated denominator.
- [ ] Keyboard reachable with visible focus, one `h1`, labelled controls, chart data available as a table, no colour-only meaning; en-CA, zh-TW, zh-CN; light and dark; 375 px and 1024 px.

### Tests

- Browser spec `frontend/tests/browser/v2-exposures.spec.ts`, reusing the 31-country case.
