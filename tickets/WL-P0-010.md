# WL-P0-010: Integrate supported ETF exposure and complete country analysis

## Goal

A user sees what they own through their ETFs: top companies, and sector, country and region shares with known and unresolved coverage, from fund holdings that refresh by themselves. This is an epic; the work is in its children.

## Context

- Children:
  - WL-P0-010A: official fund holdings stored in PostgreSQL and refreshed for held funds. After WL-P0-009B.
  - WL-P0-010B: the exposure endpoint. After WL-P0-010A and WL-P0-012A.
- The Exposures page that displays the result is WL-P0-012E, which depends on WL-P0-010B.
- Existing code the children port: `src/kurafolio/lookthrough.py`, `classification.py`, `fund_sources.py`; tests `tests/test_lookthrough.py`, `tests/test_classification.py`.
- Owner decision 2026-11-10: held ETFs get full underlying holdings, refreshed automatically, in P0. Looking up a fund the user does not hold is P1 (WL-P1-002).
- The detailed draft spec is in the local planning package `docs/specs/wealth-ledger-v2/P0/WL-P0-010.md` until WL-P0-016 publishes it. If it is not in your checkout, work from the children.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The G11 and G12 golden cases give exact US, TW, unknown and cash amounts that conserve value.
- [ ] The top-20 table ranks 1 to 20 with direct, through-fund and total share of the declared denominator.
- [ ] All countries including TW, and separate non-country buckets, remain discoverable.
- [ ] Official effective and retrieval dates and unsupported coverage survive the API and the download.

- [ ] WL-P0-010B exposure endpoint.
