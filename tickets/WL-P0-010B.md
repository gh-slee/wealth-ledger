# WL-P0-010B: Serve the V2 exposure analysis: companies, sectors, countries and regions

## Goal

The owner wants to see what they own through their ETFs: the largest companies, and the split by sector, country and region, with every country that has holdings visible, Taiwan included. The Exposures page (WL-P0-012E) needs one endpoint that returns this from live positions and the stored fund holdings. No V2 endpoint does.

## Context

### Current behaviour

- The shipped application computes look-through when a report is built and stores the result in the frozen report (`src/kurafolio/lookthrough.py:126` `calculate`, `:298` `attach_analysis`; classification in `src/kurafolio/classification.py`).
- WL-P0-001 (done) made the shipped page show every represented country.
- WL-P0-010A provides current fund holdings from PostgreSQL. WL-P0-012A provides valued positions.
- No `/api/v2` exposure route exists.

### Expected behaviour

- `GET /api/v2/analysis/exposures?currency=&denominator=securities|total` returns:
  - Top companies: rank, name, amount held directly, amount held through funds, total, and share of the denominator. The first 20 by total, with the count of the rest.
  - Sector, country and region distributions: every bucket that has an amount, no fixed list and no "Other" that hides countries. Cash, unknown, and directly held crypto are separate non-country buckets.
  - Coverage: the resolved share, the unresolved share with reasons (fund unsupported, holdings stale, child fund not resolved), and per fund the issuer, effective date and fetch time.
  - The same calculation identifier, generation, valuation time and decimal-string rules as the other analysis endpoints.
- Amounts conserve value: the buckets of each distribution sum to the denominator exactly.
- A fund inside a fund is resolved through its own holdings. A cycle or an unsupported child becomes an unresolved branch with its weight; known weights are never rescaled to 100%.
- A Bitcoin ETF is a security. Its exposure is reported as "Bitcoin through ETF", separate from directly held Bitcoin, and Total never counts an asset twice.
- A holding with no value (missing price) is excluded from amounts and listed in coverage; nothing is zero.

### Scope

- In scope: the endpoint, DTO, OpenAPI, the service that joins positions with fund holdings, tests.
- Out of scope: the page (WL-P0-012E), fund download (WL-P0-010A), overlap between funds (UX-P1-004), funds the user does not hold (WL-P1-002), exports (WL-P0-013A).

### Implementation notes

- Reuse `lookthrough.calculate` and `Identities` for recursion and company matching, and `classification.py` for sector, country and region. Their tests (`tests/test_lookthrough.py`, `tests/test_classification.py`) must pass unchanged; extend, do not fork.
- Company identity is issuer-level: two listings of one company are one row; two different companies with the same ticker on different exchanges are two rows.
- Follow the DTO and error conventions in `src/kurafolio/web_v2/schemas.py` and the decimal rules in [canonical-domain.md](../docs/canonical-domain.md).
- Cache by user, currency, denominator, fact generation, calculation generation and the fund holdings' effective dates.
- The 31-country browser case for the shipped page is `frontend/tests/browser/country-exposure.spec.ts`; give the endpoint a fixture with at least as many countries.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given an ETF worth CAD 1,000 with US 60%, TW 30%, unknown 8% and cash 2%, a direct TW stock worth CAD 200 and Bitcoin worth CAD 2,520, then countries are US 600, TW 500, unknown 80, cash 20 and direct crypto 2,520, summing to 3,720; TW is 41.67% of securities and 13.44% of total (golden case G11).
- [ ] Given fund A worth 1,000 that is 50% fund B and 50% US direct, and B is 60% TW, 20% US and 20% unknown, then TW is 300, US 600 and unknown 100; neither fund's own value is added again (golden case G12).
- [ ] Given a cycle or an unsupported child fund, then that branch is unresolved with its weight and the request finishes.
- [ ] Given 25 companies, then ranks 1 to 20 are returned with direct, through-fund and total shares of the stated denominator, and the remaining count is 5.
- [ ] Given 31 represented countries, then 31 country rows are returned.
- [ ] Given a fund whose holdings are stale, then its amounts are included and the response carries its effective date and the stale status.
- [ ] Given a Bitcoin ETF and directly held Bitcoin, then they appear in separate buckets and the total counts each once.
- [ ] Given user A, then no parameter returns user B's data.
- [ ] OpenAPI lists the route and every field; decimal values are strings.
