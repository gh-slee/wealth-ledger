# WL-P0-009: Refresh market quotes, FX and supported reporting currencies

## Goal

Values on the analysis pages follow the market: prices and FX rates are fetched at intervals and on demand for whatever users hold, the user can view everything in more currencies, and the value chart is a daily line. This is an epic; the work is in its children.

## Context

- Children, in dependency order:
  - WL-P0-009A: the quote adapter interface, a fake provider and the optional unofficial adapter. In the MVP.
  - WL-P0-009B: the PostgreSQL market store, the refresh process and the refresh and status routes. After 009A and WL-P0-012A.
  - WL-P0-009C: the display currency list and daily FX for it. After 009B.
  - WL-P0-009D: historical backfill and the daily value curve. After 009B and 009C.
  - WL-P0-009E: the official keyed provider, chosen after a spike. After 009A; in parallel with 009B to 009D.
- Each child carries its own current behaviour, expected behaviour and criteria. This epic closes when all five are done and the criteria below hold together.
- The detailed draft spec is in the local planning package `docs/specs/wealth-ledger-v2/P0/WL-P0-009.md` until WL-P0-016 publishes it. If it is not in your checkout, work from the children.

### Owner decisions

- 2026-11-10: more display currencies at the latest rate; any held stock, ETF or bond is refreshed, no fixed symbol list; a pluggable provider approach; Bitcoin from the Coinbase Exchange public API, FX from the Bank of Canada and Frankfurter, securities through an adapter with the operator's own key; every value shows its "as of" time.
- 2026-11-11: the daily curve carries the previous import's quantities forward between imports and marks those days as estimates (WL-P0-009D).
- Still open: the official provider (WL-P0-009E, after its spike) and the named unofficial endpoint (WL-P0-009A, after its spike).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A price-only refresh changes value and calculation generation without a new file or a quantity change.
- [ ] CAD, USD, TWD and the approved extra currencies reconcile to the same fixed-rate oracle on every analysis endpoint.
- [ ] A missing quote or FX rate gives a partial value, never zero or a complete-looking total.
- [ ] Manual and scheduled requests respect the provider's delay and budget and show the actual "as of" and check times.
- [ ] The value chart on Stock/Bond, Crypto and Total is daily, and estimated days are marked.
