# UX-P1-014: Make the import page show the right step, one introduction and distinguishable jobs

## Goal

A user who opens Import & jobs to add a new observation sees step 3 "Confirm and publish" marked as current, two nearly identical introductory sentences, and a job list whose rows cannot be told apart. They cannot tell where they are or which earlier import is which.

## Context

### Current behaviour

- Observed on the synthetic browser fixture on 2026-11-10: on arrival, with an earlier published import, the step list marks "3 Confirm and publish" as current while the visible form is step 1.
- `frontend/src/components/imports.tsx:170-176` derives `stage` from the selected job; a published `update` job gives 3 even though the user has not started a new draft. `:384` sets `aria-current` from it.
- Observed on the synthetic browser fixture on 2026-11-10: the page opens with two sentences that both begin "Use either supported CSV, both, ..." (`imports.tsx:393-394`, keys `uploadHint` and `importInputOptions`).
- Observed on the synthetic browser fixture on 2026-11-10: "Recent jobs" lists rows that read only "Confirm import & generate reports" and a timestamp. `imports.tsx:1040-1060` renders the job kind and `created_at` and nothing else.

### Expected behaviour

- On arrival with no draft and no job selected by the URL, step 1 is current. Selecting an earlier job shows its outcome without changing which step the draft form is on.
- One introductory paragraph that states the options and that nothing is imported before confirmation.
- Each job row shows its kind, creation time, status and valuation cutoff when the job result has one.

### Scope

- In scope: stage derivation, the introduction, job row content.
- Out of scope: the import request, preview and confirmation logic, timestamps (UX-P1-013), vocabulary such as "snapshot kind" which V2 removes (WL-P0-011D).

### Implementation notes

- Job results already carry `valuation_as_of` (`imports.tsx:678-680`).
- Status wording exists in `frontend/src/lib/evidence-status.ts:209` `jobMessage`.
- Merge the two hint keys into one in all three catalogs; delete the unused key.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given an earlier published import and no `job` parameter, when the page loads, then step 1 has `aria-current="step"`.
- [ ] Given the user selects an earlier published job, then its outcome is shown and the step list still reflects the draft form.
- [ ] Given a ready preview for the current draft, then step 2 is current; after confirming it, step 3.
- [ ] The page shows one introductory paragraph.
- [ ] Given two published imports with different cutoffs, then their rows differ in visible text and each row states its status.
- [ ] Rows remain buttons operable by keyboard with an accessible name that includes the status.

### Tests

- Extend `frontend/tests/browser/import-review.spec.ts` for criteria 1 to 3 and 5.
- Unit test for the stage function in `frontend/tests/import-review.test.ts`.
